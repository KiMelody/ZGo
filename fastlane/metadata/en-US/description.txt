ZLinker is a mobile remote for ZCode — your desktop coding agent, in your pocket.

NATIVE TASK LIST
Live tasks from all your devices: running state, latest reply preview, pinned groups. Stop, pause or resume from your phone; tap to jump straight into the conversation.

SCHEDULED AUTOMATIONS
Create, edit and toggle server-side automations from your phone: cron, fixed intervals, or one-shot delays.

OFF-PEAK TASKS
Submit work to run free when compute is idle, watch your queue position live, get notified when done. (Requires a Coding Plan subscription.)

LOCAL NOTIFICATIONS
Task finished, off-peak done, automation fired — push notifications straight to your phone, tapping one opens the conversation. The web remote can't do this.

MULTI-DEVICE
Add devices by scanning or pasting a QR link, rename, pin, export backups. Credentials never leave your phone.

PRIVACY
No data collected. No accounts, no relay servers of ours. Device links stay on your device.

ZLinker is an independent community tool, not affiliated with Zhipu AI. Only connect devices you own.
